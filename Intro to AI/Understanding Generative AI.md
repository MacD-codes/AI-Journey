# Intro to AI: Understanding Generative AI

This module focused on understanding the evolution of Natural Language Processing (NLP), the rise of Large Language Models (LLMs), and the technologies that power modern Generative AI systems.

Throughout this section, I explored how language models evolved from simple statistical methods to advanced transformer-based architectures, learned the lifecycle of building LLMs, and studied optimization techniques such as Prompt Engineering, Fine-Tuning, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
