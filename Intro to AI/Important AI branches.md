# Intro to AI: Important AI Branches

This module focused on exploring some of the most important branches of Artificial Intelligence and understanding how they contribute to real-world applications.

Throughout this section, I learned about Robotics, Computer Vision, Traditional Machine Learning, and Generative AI. I also explored how these fields combine AI techniques to solve complex problems across industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, transportation, entertainment, and business.

- Exploring the impact of Generative AI across media, healthcare, architecture, and other industries
